Azure Fundamentals Study Tools

Practice quizzes and flashcards for the Microsoft AZ-900 exam, built while studying along with an Exam Cram video course.

  • Chapter 1: Describe cloud computing 40 practice questions on cloud models, service types, the shared responsibility model, CapEx vs OpEx, and the benefits of cloud. Take them as a quiz or flip through flashcards.
  • Chapter 2: Describe Azure architecture and services 70 practice questions in four topic sets: core architectural components, compute and networking, storage, and identity, access, and security. Take them as a quiz or flip through flashcards.
  • Chapter 3: Describe Azure management and governance 60 practice questions in four topic sets: cost management, governance and compliance, managing and deploying resources, and monitoring tools. Take them as a quiz or flip through flashcards.

About these tools

Every question comes with an explanation of the right answer, and missed questions can be retried on their own.

Everything runs in your browser and nothing you answer is sent anywhere. Scores and flashcard progress are saved only in your browser's local storage.

These are study aids, not real exam questions, and they are not affiliated with or endorsed by Microsoft. Check the official Microsoft Learn skills outline for the current exam content.
