Notes

  • Cloud models: public (owned by the provider and shared by many customers), private (used by one organization), and hybrid (a mix of the two).
  • Service types: IaaS (you manage the OS and up), PaaS (you manage your apps and data), and SaaS (you use finished software). In every type the customer owns their data, identities, and devices.
  • Pricing: the cloud shifts spending from CapEx (upfront hardware) to OpEx (pay for what you use).
  • Benefits: high availability, reliability, scalability, elasticity, predictability, security, governance, manageability, and economies of scale.
  • Serverless: event-driven code on servers the provider manages, billed per execution (Azure Functions is the usual example).
